<objective>
Fix two confirmed P1 findings from the `/impeccable clarify` design-review pass on the main
dashboard (`.impeccable/critique/2026-08-27T08-27-13Z__frontend-src-app-tsx.md`): (1) the BP
Timeline hero chart's Elevated and Stage 1 AHA band labels visually collide because those two

corner-legend, no point aggregation/downsampling (both explicitly deferred as larger follow-ups).

read of Chris's own BP trend) without touching any locked clinical/chart-series color token
(DESIGN.md's "Data-Is-Locked Rule").

Output: a new `isDotCrowded` predicate + tests in `frontend/src/lib`, a new `useElementWidth` hook,
and updated `BPTimeline.tsx`/`PulseTrend.tsx` wiring -- committed in one commit.
</objective>
