<objective>
Fix the layout-grouping finding from the impeccable audit of `frontend/src/App.tsx`'s `Dashboard()`: `<main>` currently applies one flat `gap-8` (32px) between all six top-level children (FilterBar, OverlayToggle, StatsStrip, the chart region, the ReadingsTable section, OverlayEventsList), so a squint test reveals six equally-weighted slabs instead of three meaningful zones. Group the six children into three semantic wrapper `<div>`s — filter controls, data visualizations, detail/record surfaces — with a tighter gap-4 inside the controls cluster and gap-8 inside the other two, then raise `<main>`'s own gap (now only spanning BETWEEN the three groups) to gap-12 (48px), DESIGN.md's documented "2xl / major section breaks" token, which is currently defined but unused anywhere in the codebase.

Purpose: Visual hierarchy should communicate grouping (which controls/content belong together vs. where a real content break happens) through spacing weight alone — CardioStream's own design system already names this scale (`md`=16px for tight clusters, `xl`=32px for related-but-distinct content, `2xl`=48px for major section breaks), it just was not being applied hierarchically before this fix.

Output: `frontend/src/App.tsx` — same 6 rendered children, same props, same classNames on every one of them; only 3 new wrapper `<div>`s added and `<main>`'s className changed from `gap-8` to `gap-12`. No color, typography, copy, breakpoint, or interactive-behavior change anywhere.
</objective>
