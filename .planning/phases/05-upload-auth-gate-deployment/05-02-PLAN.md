| T-05-02 | Spoofing/Tampering | Bearer token forgery | mitigate | itsdangerous `URLSafeTimedSerializer` signature; `BadData` → 401; `TOKEN_SECRET` from env only, never shipped to client. Verified by tampered-token 401 test. |
| T-05-03 | Information Disclosure | timing attack on password | mitigate | `hmac.compare_digest` (source assertion: `compare_digest` present in routers/auth.py). |
