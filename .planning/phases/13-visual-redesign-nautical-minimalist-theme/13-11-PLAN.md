No `XAxis`/`YAxis`/`Tooltip`/`CartesianGrid` — this mirrors `PulseTrend.tsx`'s mini-variant gating (no axes, no tooltip) but is simpler since this component has no "hero" branch at all — it is always the minimal decorative shape.
