# Phase 16.1 Plan 08: Vitals Strip Summary

The 588px stat-card block plus category-percent list becomes one ~90px mist island of four two-line readouts, with the visible range shortened to `98–142` so the row holds its height at the 1024px worst case while the min/max words survive verbatim in an `sr-only` span.

## What shipped

**Task 1 — `8aaab41`** `StatsStrip.tsx` rewritten end to end. Props narrowed to `{ stats, isLoading }`; the `Reading` type import, the `readings` prop, the four lucide tile icons, the sparkline, the latest-category status pill and the six-chip category-percent list (with its `categoryColor` / `CHIP_TEXT` imports) are all gone. The surface is one `rounded-xl bg-[var(--color-mist)] px-6 py-3 shadow-[var(--shadow-elevation)]` row holding `grid grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-4 gap-4`. Each vitals cell is two line boxes: the label `<p>` at `text-label`, then a baseline-aligned `flex gap-2` row carrying the value at `text-display` and the range `<p>` at `text-base`. `StatsStrip.test.tsx` created with 16 tests.

**Task 2 — `e5ff714`** `charts/StatsSparkline.tsx` and `charts/StatsSparkline.test.tsx` deleted — the strip was their only consumer. `CombinedTimeline.test.tsx`'s header comment now states the jsdom/`ResponsiveContainer` fact directly instead of pointing at the deleted test file, in the same commit as the deletion. Its dashed-pulse-stroke assertion is untouched.

## Load-bearing details

**The range element.** `<p className="text-base …">` containing an `aria-hidden="true"` span with `` `${min}–${max}` `` (U+2013, no spaces) and an `sr-only` span with `` `minimum ${min}, maximum ${max}` ``. A visually-hidden span rather than `aria-label` on the `<p>`, because `aria-label` on a generic-role element is not reliably honoured. The test asserts the en dash **by codepoint** (`"98–142"`) and separately asserts `queryByText("98-142")` and `queryByText("min 98 · max 142")` are both null, so a hyphen substitution or a revert to the long form fails a gate rather than silently wrapping the cell to a third line.

**The divider is in the gap, not on the cell.** Preflight sets `box-sizing: border-box`, so `lg:border-l-2` would have consumed 2px of the 156.0px cell at 1024px, taking §9.4's 9.9px of slack down to 7.9px — a silent contradiction of the figure the spec table rests on. The divider is instead `lg:relative lg:before:absolute lg:before:inset-y-0 lg:before:-left-2 lg:before:w-0.5 lg:before:bg-[var(--color-depth)] lg:before:content-['']`, painted 8px into the 16px gap at zero content-width cost. Verified against the built CSS: the rules emit inside `@media (min-width:64rem)` immediately after `.lg\:grid-cols-4`.

**One section, one landmark.** The old component rendered `<section aria-label="Summary statistics">` twice (loading branch and data branch). It is now a single section with `aria-busy={isLoading}` and the branch inside the grid, which is both cleaner and what makes the plan's `grep -c` pins of `1` satisfiable.

**The Readings cell.** Label plus `stats.count`, two line boxes, nothing in the secondary slot. Two tests guard it: the cell's `textContent` is exactly `"Readings30"`, and it contains none of `total` / `all time` / `minimum` / `maximum`.

**The null contract (D-22).** `count === 0` renders six em dashes (three values, three ranges) and three `sr-only` "minimum and maximum unavailable" spans. The test additionally asserts the **only** `0` in the whole strip is the reading count itself, and that no vitals cell's `textContent` contains `0` — so a regression that printed `0` for a missing average fails rather than reading as a clinical fact.
